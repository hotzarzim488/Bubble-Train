<a id="overview"></a>
Bubble Train is a captivating puzzle game that offers a complete package for fans of Zuma. With its full version available for free download, players can enjoy all features and updates without any limitations. This game invites players to clear all bubbles of the same color by shooting them from a cannon, creating groups of three or more to eliminate them before they reach the end of their path. 

Offering a unique level editor, Bubble Train allows you to craft your own challenges, ensuring both easy and difficult gameplay experiences. Whether you're a seasoned Zuma player or a newcomer, Bubble Train caters to all skill levels with adjustable difficulty settings.
